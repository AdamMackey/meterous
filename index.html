In your toolbar

The badge is your current session. The ring fills with your weekly usage.

Three meters, one glance

Current session, Fable, and weekly across all models. The toolbar badge shows your session without opening anything.

Know when it resets

Every meter shows its reset time. Colors follow claude.ai's own warning levels, so amber and red mean what they mean there.

Private by design

No servers, no analytics, no access to your conversations. It reads your usage numbers with the login already in Safari.

See it in action

Eight seconds, sound on: open Safari, click the icon, read your meters.

Set up in a minute

  1. Open Meterous once from your Applications folder.
  2. In Safari, choose Settings → Extensions and tick Meterous.
  3. Click the Meterous icon in the toolbar and allow it to use claude.ai.
  4. That's it. Click the icon any time to see your meters.

Questions?
